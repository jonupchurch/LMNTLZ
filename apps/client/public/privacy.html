Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 July 2026

LMNTLZ is operated by Gravytraining, based in Ohio, United States, and we are the data controller for everything described here. This policy says what we collect, why, who else touches it, and how long we keep it.

We are a US business with players everywhere, so the data below is stored and processed in the United States. If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, the sections on legal bases, transfers and your rights explain how that works and what you can ask of us.

Two things worth saying at the top. We never see your card details — our reseller Paddle handles payment and we receive only the fact that an order completed. And the game sets no cookies at all — not for sign-in, not for counting visits, not for advertising. Your session token is held in memory and is gone when you close the tab. If you sign in, one item is kept in your browser's local storage: a renewal token, so that you do not have to sign in again for thirty days. Signing out deletes it.

What we collect

Account

  • Your identity provider's subject ID and email address. When you sign in with Google, Google tells us a permanent ID for you and the email on the account. We store both. We never receive your Google password.
  • The username you choose, and a record of username changes.
  • Session and renewal tokens, so you stay signed in. Renewal tokens are stored server-side and rotate on use.

Gameplay

  • Your squads — which champions you have placed where, and how you have configured them.
  • A record of every battle: who fought, the outcome, how many turns it took, which squads were used, whether the defender was a bot, and your league at the time.
  • Replays — the event log of a battle, so it can be watched back.
  • Guild membership, and chat messages you send.

Storing is not showing. We record which champions were in a squad because balancing the game is impossible without it. That does not mean it is visible to other players: your Hidden defense squad is never shown to anyone, and the data export described below deliberately omits squad composition so that exporting your own history cannot become a way to scout somebody else's.

Purchases

  • What you bought and when it expires. We hold the entitlement, an order reference, and the fact that a payment succeeded, was refunded, or was reversed.
  • We do not hold your card number, billing address or tax details. Paddle does, as merchant of record.

Technical

  • IP address and request metadata, in server logs, used to rate-limit, to investigate abuse, and to keep the service up.
  • Error reports when something breaks, which can include the page you were on and what the client was doing.
  • Anonymous visit counts. We count page views so we know how many people reach the site and roughly where they arrived from. This uses Vercel Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and builds no profile that follows you between sites: a visit is identified by a one-way hash of the request, which is discarded after 24 hours. It records the page, the referring site, and coarse details like country, browser and whether you are on a desktop. Before a page view is recorded we cut the address down to the page itself, so anything a link carried after it — a sign-in code, a search term — is removed before it leaves your browser. A visit can never be connected to your account.

The record that a battle happened, and its resultIndefinitely — see below
Chat messagesA rolling window, and longer where a message has been reported

Server logsA short rolling window

Why battle records are kept indefinitely. Rankings, matchmaking and every balance decision are computed from the history of battles actually played. That history cannot be reconstructed later: a battle not recorded at the time is gone. Deleting your account removes your identity from these records rather than deleting the records themselves — what remains is not linked to you.

Your rights

We give every player the same rights, wherever they live. Sorting players by which privacy law happens to cover them would cost more to build than simply honouring the request. You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or hand it to someone else. To do any of these, contact us — we will respond within one month, and sooner where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising — as those terms are defined under California law and its equivalents in other US states. There is no opt-out to find because there is nothing to opt out of. We will not discriminate against you for making any request described here.

  • A copy of your data is also available from your profile as a CSV you can download yourself, without asking.
  • Deleting your account removes your identity, email, username, squads and guild membership. Battle records are anonymised as described above, and purchase records are kept where tax law requires.

If you think we have got this wrong, tell us first — we would rather fix it. You can also complain to a regulator: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EEA, your national data protection authority; in the United States, your state Attorney General.

Children

LMNTLZ is not for children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and the UK. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Renewal tokens are stored hashed and rotate on every use, so a stolen one stops working as soon as the real session continues. Access to production data is limited to the people who run the game. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected players and the relevant authority if a breach requires it.

Changes

We will update this policy as the game grows. The date at the top changes when we do, and we will announce material changes in the game.
