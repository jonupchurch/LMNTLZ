What to include

The more of this you can give us, the faster it goes — but send the message either way, and we will ask for what is missing.

  • Your in-game username. Not your email — usernames are what we can look up.
  • For anything about a purchase, the order reference from your Paddle receipt.
  • For a bug, what you were doing, what happened, and roughly when.
  • For a report about another player, their username and what they did. Use the in-game report button where there is one — it attaches the evidence automatically.
