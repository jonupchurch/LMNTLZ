Refund Policy

Last updated 29 July 2026

Fourteen days, no questions asked. If you bought a pass within the last 14 days and you want your money back, ask and you will get it. The pass is removed from your account when the refund is issued.

What can be refunded

LMNTLZ sells one thing — a boost pass, in seven durations. That is the only purchase there is, and it is refundable in full for 14 days from the date of purchase.

You do not need a reason, and we do not require the pass to be unused. When the refund is issued the entitlement is revoked: the remaining time comes off your account. Any shards you already earned while the pass was active stay with you.

This is more than the law requires in most places. Under UK and EU consumer law the 14-day right to change your mind normally ends the moment you agree to start using digital content immediately — we do not rely on that, and we give you the 14 days anyway.

After 14 days

We do not offer refunds on pass time outside that window as a matter of course, because a pass is consumed day by day and most of it will have been used. Ask anyway if something went wrong — a duplicate charge, a purchase you did not make, a pass that was not granted, a technical failure that cost you the days you paid for. Those are fixed regardless of the date.

If we close your account for a reason other than breaking the terms, or if we shut the service down, you can ask for a proportionate refund of unused pass time. If we close your account because you broke the terms, unused time is not refunded.

What is not refundable

  • Shards. They cannot be bought, so there is nothing to refund.
  • Progress, rankings, or items you earned by playing. They were never sold to you.

How to ask

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com, who is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle handles returns, so there are two routes and both work:

  1. Reply to your Paddle receipt, or use the link on it to contact Paddle directly. This is the fastest route.
  2. Contact us with the order reference from your receipt and we will arrange it with Paddle.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Paddle usually processes them within a few business days; how quickly it appears after that is up to your bank, and can take another week.

Chargebacks

Please ask us before you ask your bank. A chargeback costs us a fee on top of the refund and takes far longer to resolve for you. We will refund you inside the window without argument, so there is nothing to be gained by it. Accounts with an unresolved chargeback may be suspended until it is settled.

Your statutory rights

Nothing here removes any right you have under consumer law where you live, including rights relating to digital content that is faulty, not as described, or not fit for purpose.
