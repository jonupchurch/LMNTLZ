# Claude Design Prompt — Brand Identity

> **How to use:** Paste the `## PROMPT` block into Claude Design. This is the outward-facing brand system — logo, marks, brand color, voice, applications. It pairs with `design-system.md` (the in-app UI kit) and expands the brand half of `00-style-system.md`. Generate this before or alongside the design system so the wordmark and brand colors are settled.

## PROMPT

Design a complete **brand identity page** for **LMNTLZ** (pronounced "elementals"), a competitive fantasy battler game. The identity must match the game's established art direction: **stylized / semi-anime**, bold and confident, a little arcane and premium — the same world the character portraits live in. Deliver a single cohesive brand-guidelines page containing:

**1. Logo / wordmark suite.** The name is stylized with the vowels removed — make that a deliberate feature, not a typo. Present a primary wordmark plus variants:
- A **primary wordmark** — the strongest concept, refined.
- **2–3 alternate directions** to choose from: one glyph-forward (letterforms built from elemental motifs — a flame-crossed T, a crescent for the L, etc.), one clean geometric/gaming, one etched/rune-like.
- **Lockups:** horizontal, stacked, and a compact one-line version.
- **Monochrome:** solid black and solid white (knockout) versions that still read.

**2. Brand mark / monogram / app icon.** A standalone sigil that works with no wordmark — an "L" built from an elemental sigil, or a unifying emblem that suggests "the nine forces / six-to-strike-six-to-stand." Show it as a rounded-square **app icon** on a dark arcane ground, and as a small favicon/avatar. It must be legible at 32px.

**3. Brand color system — "the Elemental Spectrum."** The nine type colors ARE the brand palette; treat them as a spectrum, not nine random swatches. Present them grouped as the 6-magic ring and 3-melee triangle, with hex:
- Earth `#4E7C3A` · Air `#8FCFE0` · Fire `#E8552B` · Water `#2A7FB8` · Light `#F2C744` · Dark `#7A3FA0` · Slash `#C0313A` · Pierce `#3B5BD1` · Crush `#B5732E`.
- Define **brand neutrals / grounds:** deep arcane indigo-charcoal `#141221` → `#241F38`, plus an ivory/parchment for light contexts.
- Nominate a **primary brand accent** (recommend gold `#F2C744` as the "hero/CTA" color, since it reads as premium and belongs to Light) and a secondary. Show a brand gradient built from the spectrum for hero moments.

**4. Brand typography.** A characterful **display face** for the wordmark, headlines, and hero names; a clean, highly legible **support face** for body, UI, and numerals. Show a brand type specimen with pairing rules and a scale.

**5. Brand voice & messaging.** A short voice descriptor (confident, mythic, a little wry — see the lore), 3–5 **tagline options** drawn from the world (e.g. "Send six to strike. Leave six to stand." / "Every strength is a locked door." / "No force rules."), and a one-line elevator description. Keep it to a tidy panel.

**6. Iconography language.** The nine element sigils as one consistent icon family (Earth crystal/leaf, Air spiral/feather, Fire flame, Water droplet/wave, Light burst, Dark crescent, Slash blade-streak, Pierce spearpoint, Crush impact), shown at badge size.

**7. Usage & applications.** Clear-space and minimum-size rules; correct vs. incorrect logo usage (don't stretch, recolor off-palette, add effects); the logo on light, on dark, and over character key art. Then mockup a few **applications** so the brand feels real: app icon, store feature banner / hero key-art lockup, loading screen, and a social avatar + header.

Lay it out as a polished, self-contained brand book — cohesive with the semi-anime, dark-arcane world of the game.
