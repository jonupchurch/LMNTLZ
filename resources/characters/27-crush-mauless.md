# Mauless — "The Undenied" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Crush — Martial (melee)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Crush read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Crush *(primary)* · Pierce *(second discipline — melee)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Crush · Pierce  
> **Weak to:** Slash  
> *Pure-martial specialist — no Light/Dark attunement; strong against two melee forms, with a single opening. he drives straight through the guard that beats Crush — but a clean edge undoes him.*

## Concept
A relentless siege-breaker for whom guards break first and then everything under them; he refuses to be stopped.

## Build & silhouette
Big and forward-leaning, a battering-ram build, hunched with unstoppable drive.

## Face & demeanor
Set-jawed and teeth-bared with tunnel-vision determination; scarred and sweat-sheened, without a trace of fear.

## Wardrobe & materials
Battered bronze siege-armor with ram-and-breach motifs, a great shield-face like a battering head; chains and reinforcement plates.

## Weapon & props
A ram-shield and heavy gauntlet-fists, or a breaching maul — he shatters guards and walls alike.

## Element expression
Guards and shields shatter in a burst of bronze impact-shards, a breach-crack spidering outward through the dust of broken defense.

## Palette
Forged bronze `#B5732E` with `#E0A15A` accents; iron-grey and rust. Heavy matte metal and dust.

## Key-art pose & mood
Shoulder-first through a shattering shield-wall, bronze impact-shards flying, driving straight through.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** emphasize relentless forward drive and breaking-through imagery — unstoppable.
- **Avoid:** hesitant or defensive. He only ever goes forward.
