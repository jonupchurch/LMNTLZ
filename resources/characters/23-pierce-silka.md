# Silka Pinquick · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Pierce — Martial (melee)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Pierce read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Pierce *(primary)* · Dark *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Pierce · Dark  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Slash  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Light  
> *Attunement: she works from the shadows.*

## Concept
A tiny, blurring-fast skirmisher who's already behind you; a needle-quick trickster.

## Build & silhouette
Small and wiry and coiled, kept low and quick; impish.

## Face & demeanor
A sharp grin and glinting eyes, quick and cheeky; cropped hair and a smudge of grime.

## Wardrobe & materials
Close-fitting cobalt scout's gear festooned with slim throwing-spikes and needles; buckles and quick-draw bandoliers.

## Weapon & props
A cluster of needle-daggers and throwing pins — many small, precise punctures.

## Element expression
A scatter of thin cobalt puncture-lines from several angles at once, an after-image blur of speed, pinpoint sparks.

## Palette
Cobalt `#3B5BD1` with `#7B93F0` accents; steel-blue. Convergent lines and a single bright point.

## Key-art pose & mood
Mid-dart, already past the viewer's guard, needles flashing, a blur-trail behind her.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** lean on speed, small stature, and a multiplicity of points — playful menace.
- **Avoid:** bulky or slow. She's a blur of pins.
