# Corvane — "Shepherd of Endings" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Dark — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Dark read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Dark *(primary)* · Earth *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Dark · Earth  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Light  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Air  
> *Attunement: the grave and the soil that receives the dead.*

## Concept
A somber psychopomp who knows the appointed hour of every soul on the field; death's quiet clerk.

## Build & silhouette
Tall and lean and cloaked, with a shepherd's stoop and a staff in hand; patient.

## Face & demeanor
Grave and ageless, a hooded shadow over pale features, knowing dark eyes; a raven on the shoulder.

## Wardrobe & materials
A long dark-violet shepherd's cloak marked with tally-strokes and hourglass motifs; a dim soul-lantern; a ragged hem.

## Weapon & props
A shepherd's crook that doubles as a scythe-staff, and a soul-lantern; he herds shadows and marks the doomed.

## Element expression
Dim violet soul-wisps gather to him, a lantern of pale ghost-light glows, ravens and drifting motes of the departed circle.

## Palette
Arcane violet `#7A3FA0` with magenta `#C64BB0` edges; near-black voids. Let shadow eat detail.

## Key-art pose & mood
Raising a dim soul-lantern amid drifting wisps, crook in hand, quietly counting the field.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** keep him solemn, patient, and pastoral — muted violet gloom.
- **Avoid:** aggressive or flashy. He simply arrives on time.
