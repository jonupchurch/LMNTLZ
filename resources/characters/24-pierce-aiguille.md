# Lord Aiguille — "The Long Point" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Pierce — Martial (melee)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Pierce read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Pierce *(primary)* · Light *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Pierce · Light  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Slash  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Dark  
> *Attunement: aristocratic brilliance and polish.*

## Concept
A haughty rapier-noble who reaches you before you've decided to move; aristocratic range.

## Build & silhouette
Tall and slender, imperiously straight, with long-limbed elegance.

## Face & demeanor
Aloof and aquiline, a thin superior smile beneath lidded eyes; immaculate, with fine waxed features.

## Wardrobe & materials
Ornate cobalt-and-silver court-duelist's finery — long tails, lace at the cuffs, a single fine pauldron; pristine.

## Weapon & props
An extraordinarily long, fine estoc or rapier for outreaching every opponent.

## Element expression
A very long, thin cobalt thrust-line extends improbably far to a glinting pinpoint at extreme range.

## Palette
Cobalt `#3B5BD1` with `#7B93F0` accents; steel-blue. Convergent lines and a single bright point.

## Key-art pose & mood
A languid, extended thrust reaching across a great distance, poised and entirely unbothered — one long cobalt line.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** lean on reach, refinement, and aristocratic distance — extreme length.
- **Avoid:** scrappy or close-quarters. He never lets you close.
