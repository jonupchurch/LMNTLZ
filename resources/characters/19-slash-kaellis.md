# Kaellis — "The Duelist Immaculate" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Slash — Martial (melee)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Slash read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Slash *(primary)* · Light *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Slash · Light  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Crush  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Dark  
> *Attunement: an honorable duelist's radiant discipline.*

## Concept
A flawless swordsman who has never needed a second stroke; precision and pride made a person.

## Build & silhouette
Trim, elegant, and upright — a perfect duelist's posture, not bulky.

## Face & demeanor
Refined and composed with a faint superior calm; sharp, groomed features; perhaps a single clean scar.

## Wardrobe & materials
An immaculate crimson-and-steel duelist's coat, high collar, a single pauldron, a sash of rank; spotless and tailored.

## Weapon & props
One flawless longsword or saber, drawn with ceremony — a single clean cut.

## Element expression
A crimson after-image arc marks the perfect cut — a single bright slash-line across the frame, no wasted motion.

## Palette
Crimson steel `#C0313A` with `#F06A72` accents; polished steel and blood-red. Sharp, clean edges.

## Key-art pose & mood
Sword extended (or just resheathed) after one stroke, a crimson cut-line hanging in the air behind the target.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** lean on precision, poise, and cleanliness — one decisive line.
- **Avoid:** messy, wild, or over-armored. He's a scalpel.
