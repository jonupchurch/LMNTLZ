# Lucen — "The Unhidden" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Light — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Light read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Light *(primary)* · Air *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Light · Air  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Dark  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Earth  
> *Attunement: clarity carried on clean light through open air.*

## Concept
A revealer who strips away illusions, wards, and secrets alike; light as pure exposure.

## Build & silhouette
Lean and sharp, scholarly-agile, with an alert, probing posture.

## Face & demeanor
Keen, searching eyes that glow faintly, a knowing half-smile; clean features and light-streaked hair.

## Wardrobe & materials
A sleek gold-and-white inquisitor's coat with lens and prism motifs; a monocle or ocular device; layered but light.

## Weapon & props
A prism-lantern or light-lens that projects revealing beams, and blades of focused light.

## Element expression
Beams that pierce and dispel, illusions peeling away in the glare, refracted rainbows, things made suddenly transparent.

## Palette
Radiant gold `#F2C744` with ivory `#FFF6D6`; warm whites. Strong bloom and halo, hard cast shadows.

## Key-art pose & mood
Raising a prism-lantern, a fan of revealing light sweeping out, shadows and illusions dissolving.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** lean on clarity and precision optics — clean radiant beams.
- **Avoid:** murky or over-ornate. He's about exposure and clarity.
