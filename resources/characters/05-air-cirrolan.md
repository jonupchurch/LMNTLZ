# Cirrolan — "Whisper of the High Reach" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Air — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Air read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Air *(primary)* · Water *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Air · Water  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Earth  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Fire  
> *Attunement: cloud and mist are wind made visible; she rides the vapor.*

## Concept
A sky-borne herald who carries rumors and storms in the same breath; a trickster messenger.

## Build & silhouette
Slender and buoyant, seeming to hover; youthful and androgynous.

## Face & demeanor
Mischievous, quick-grinning, wind-tousled; a scarf that never quite settles across the mouth.

## Wardrobe & materials
A courier's harness and layered wind-cloak of pale cyan, feathers and paper charms tucked in; a long streaming scarf and loose wraps.

## Weapon & props
No melee weapon — flings gusts and cyclone-borne messages; perhaps a signal-horn or bell that summons wind.

## Element expression
Swirls of cloud-vapor and drifting feathers; papers and leaves caught in updrafts; a soft silver storm-glow.

## Palette
Pale cyan `#8FCFE0` with silver-white `#DCE6EA`; high-value, low-saturation, misty. Keep it airy and weightless.

## Key-art pose & mood
Alighting on a gust, cloak billowing into a near-cloud, one hand cupped as if about to release the wind.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** keep them light, aerial, and playful, with lots of drifting elements.
- **Avoid:** menacing or armored. They're a breeze with a secret.
