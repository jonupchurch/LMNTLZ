# Zephyrine — "The Thin Blade of Wind" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Air — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Air read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Air *(primary)* · Light *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Air · Light  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Earth  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Dark  
> *Attunement: high, clear skies — her wind carries the dawn.*

## Concept
A wind-duelist who fights at a range no one can close, cutting with compressed air.

## Build & silhouette
Lithe and tall with elongated limbs and a fencer's poise. Weightless-looking.

## Face & demeanor
Sharp, fine features and a cool half-smile; pale eyes; hair streaming as if always in a private breeze.

## Wardrobe & materials
Layered silk and gauze in cyan and silver that trail like vapor; minimal armor, a high collar, long ribbons that read her every movement.

## Weapon & props
A slender rapier or a war-fan that shears the air — but her true weapon is the wind-blade the motion leaves behind.

## Element expression
Slivers of compressed wind trail her strokes; ribbons and hair are always in motion; faint whistling spiral-lines cut the air.

## Palette
Pale cyan `#8FCFE0` with silver-white `#DCE6EA`; high-value, low-saturation, misty. Keep it airy and weightless.

## Key-art pose & mood
Mid-lunge, ribbons streaming, a crescent of wind-cut air slicing forward.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** keep it elegant, aloof, and ever-moving — lean on negative space and flowing line.
- **Avoid:** heavy, grounded, or a cluttered silhouette.
