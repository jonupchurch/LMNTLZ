# Tidewarden Coll · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Water — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Water read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Water *(primary)* · Earth *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Water · Earth  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Fire  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Air  
> *Attunement: the stone seawall — coast is water meeting land.*

## Concept
A stoic coastal bulwark who yields ground only to take a coastline; a defensive juggernaut.

## Build & silhouette
Big and solid, barrel-chested — a breakwater given a body, with a planted stance.

## Face & demeanor
Stern and salt-weathered, beard beaded with brine, steady blue eyes; calm resolve.

## Wardrobe & materials
Heavy blue-green scale-and-plate like a ship's hull, barnacled pauldrons, a warden's tabard; a kelp cloak.

## Weapon & props
A great tower shield shaped like a cresting wave and a boarding-maul; walls of water rise at his command.

## Element expression
Water sheets off his shield, a bulwark-wave curls up behind him, and foam gathers at his feet.

## Palette
Deep azure `#2A7FB8` with `#4FC3D6` accents; teal-greens and pearl. Wet sheen, translucency, slow drift.

## Key-art pose & mood
Braced behind his wave-shield as a wall of water curls overhead — holding the line.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** make him an immovable coastal defender: nautical and weighty.
- **Avoid:** agile or slender. He's a seawall.
