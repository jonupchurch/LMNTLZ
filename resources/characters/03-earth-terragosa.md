# Terragosa — "The Green Crown" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Earth — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Earth read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Earth *(primary)* · Light *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Earth · Light  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Air  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Dark  
> *Attunement: sunlight feeds her endless overgrowth.*

## Concept
A regal druid-queen who turns devastation into overgrowth; her mercy looks like a slow siege of roots.

## Build & silhouette
Stately and statuesque, a commanding matriarch. Upright and crowned.

## Face & demeanor
Serene, weather-beautiful, with bark-textured skin at the temples and eyes the green-gold of sunlit canopy. A crown of living antler-branches.

## Wardrobe & materials
Flowing moss-green and amber robes embroidered with vine-work that seems to still be growing; a mantle of leaves, orchard-blossom in her hair.

## Weapon & props
A living staff that buds and flowers; she commands thorns, roots, and blooming brambles.

## Element expression
Flowers open in her wake, roots erupt to bind foes, and amber pollen-light drifts around her.

## Palette
Mossy jade `#4E7C3A` with amber `#C9922E` accents; stone-greys and root-browns. Amber is the 'power' glow that wakes in the cracks.

## Key-art pose & mood
Arm outstretched, a wave of thorned vines surging forward, blossoms opening amid the ruin.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** make her majestic, verdant, and relentless — beauty used as a weapon.
- **Avoid:** dainty or purely gentle. Her overgrowth is a siege.
