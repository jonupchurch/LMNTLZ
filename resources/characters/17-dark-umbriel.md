# Umbriel — "The Undoing" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Dark — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Dark read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Dark *(primary)* · Fire *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Dark · Fire  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Light  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Water  
> *Attunement: unmaking as a cold, consuming burn.*

## Concept
A reality-eroding void-mage who unwrites what should never have been; entropy incarnate.

## Build & silhouette
Shifting and indistinct at the edges, tall, with parts of the silhouette seeming to be missing or dissolving.

## Face & demeanor
Half-erased — features fading into void on one side; a single calm, vast violet eye; the rest of him unraveling.

## Wardrobe & materials
Tattered void-robes whose hems dissolve into nothing, star-flecked darkness within, magenta cracks of unmaking running through.

## Weapon & props
Zones of erasure — he reaches out and things simply cease; a hand trailing dissolving matter.

## Element expression
The world frays where he gestures — particle-dissolution, negative space eating the frame, magenta rifts opening.

## Palette
Arcane violet `#7A3FA0` with magenta `#C64BB0` edges; near-black voids. Let shadow eat detail.

## Key-art pose & mood
One hand outstretched, reality unraveling into void-flecks around it, half his own form dissolving.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** lean on entropy and unsettling incompleteness — use negative space.
- **Avoid:** solid, defined, or symmetrical. He is coming apart.
