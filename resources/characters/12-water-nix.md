# Nix — "of the Still Pool" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Water — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Water read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Water *(primary)* · Air *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Water · Air  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Fire  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Earth  
> *Attunement: the still, breathless calm of the mirror-pool.*

## Concept
Perfect, dangerous calm — an assassin-mage of motionless water; the last thing you see is the ripple.

## Build & silhouette
Slight, silent, and poised, with a minimal, economical stance.

## Face & demeanor
Blank serenity, glassy still eyes, wet-smooth pale skin; hair flat and dark like a mirror surface.

## Wardrobe & materials
Sleek, close-fitting deep-teal garb with mirror-still water motifs and a single reflective sash; understated.

## Weapon & props
Blades of frozen-still water or mirror-shards; strikes from perfect stillness with a single lethal ripple.

## Element expression
Mirror-flat water surfaces form around them and then shatter into shards; one expanding ripple marks a strike.

## Palette
Deep azure `#2A7FB8` with `#4FC3D6` accents; teal-greens and pearl. Wet sheen, translucency, slow drift.

## Key-art pose & mood
Standing on a mirror-still water surface, one blade just drawn, a single ripple spreading — everything else motionless.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** lean on eerie stillness and mirror imagery — the tension of a held breath.
- **Avoid:** turbulent or emotive. Nix is a calm surface over a long drop.
