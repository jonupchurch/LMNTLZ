# Nyxara — "The Kind Veil" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Dark — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Dark read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Dark *(primary)* · Water *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Dark · Water  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Light  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Fire  
> *Attunement: her veils fall like a soft tide.*

## Concept
A gentle executioner — an elegant shadow-witch who is merciful about the endings she brings.

## Build & silhouette
Tall and graceful, with a dancer's calm and an unhurried menace.

## Face & demeanor
Beautiful and tranquil, half-lidded with something like compassion; violet eyes, dark hair, a soft sad smile behind a gauzy veil.

## Wardrobe & materials
A flowing violet-black gown of drifting veil-layers with magenta undertones catching the light; subtle raven and moth motifs.

## Weapon & props
Ribbons of shadow and a veil that becomes a blade; a smothering darkness that is almost tender.

## Element expression
Darkness pools and drifts like veils, magenta-edged tendrils reaching, moths or petals of dark light stirring.

## Palette
Arcane violet `#7A3FA0` with magenta `#C64BB0` edges; near-black voids. Let shadow eat detail.

## Key-art pose & mood
Extending a veil of shadow gently forward, as if drawing a curtain closed over a foe. Serene.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** lean on elegance and tenderness with quiet menace — soft dark drapery.
- **Avoid:** monstrous or grotesque. Her horror is her kindness.
