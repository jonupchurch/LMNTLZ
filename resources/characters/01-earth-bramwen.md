# Bramwen — "The Slow Wrath" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Earth — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Earth read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Earth *(primary)* · Fire *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Earth · Fire  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Air  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Water  
> *Attunement: the magma deep beneath the stone — her slow wrath runs molten.*

## Concept
A stone-warden whose fury is geological — it arrives late and never recedes.

## Build & silhouette
Broad and monolithic, heavier at the base than the top; reads like a standing menhir given shoulders. An older woman with an immovable stance.

## Face & demeanor
Weathered and deeply lined like cracked bedrock; heavy brow, half-lidded calm that hasn't yet decided to be angry. Moss in the creases.

## Wardrobe & materials
Layered slate-plate and packed-earth robes, faint amber resin glowing along the seams; a mantle of lichen and root over the shoulders.

## Weapon & props
No blade — her fists and a slab-like tower of raw strata she uses as a shield. She fights by simply being in the way.

## Element expression
Amber light spiders across her stone skin when she wakes; small stones orbit her slowly and the ground fractures where she plants a foot.

## Palette
Mossy jade `#4E7C3A` with amber `#C9922E` accents; stone-greys and root-browns. Amber is the 'power' glow that wakes in the cracks.

## Key-art pose & mood
Standing utterly still mid-battlefield as rubble settles around her, one fist just lowered from an impact, dust rising.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** lean all the way into 'immovable geology' — weight everything downward.
- **Avoid:** nimble, quick, or ornate. She is not a knight, she's a mountain.
