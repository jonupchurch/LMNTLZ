# Ossic — "Keeper of Bones" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Earth — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Earth read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Earth *(primary)* · Dark *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Earth · Dark  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Air  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Light  
> *Attunement: he communes with god-bone and the dead earth; the grave is close kin.*

## Concept
A gaunt geomancer who speaks to the god-bone buried under the shard-isles and raises walls from it.

## Build & silhouette
Tall, spindly, slightly stooped — a dowsing-rod of a man. Wiry and ascetic.

## Face & demeanor
Hollow-cheeked, eyes milky-pale like fossil, wearing a serene listening expression as if hearing something underground. Close-shorn, with carved bone studs.

## Wardrobe & materials
Ragged earth-brown wrappings hung with fossil fragments, teeth, and vertebrae talismans; one heavy relic gauntlet on the digging hand.

## Weapon & props
A crook-staff of fused vertebrae. He conjures rising slabs and bone-pillars from the ground rather than striking directly.

## Element expression
Pale amber runes crawl up the bones that erupt at his call; dust and sediment swirl toward his open hand.

## Palette
Mossy jade `#4E7C3A` with amber `#C9922E` accents; stone-greys and root-browns. Amber is the 'power' glow that wakes in the cracks.

## Key-art pose & mood
Kneeling with one hand pressed to the ground, a jagged wall of bone-stone heaving up behind him.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** make him eerie, reverent, and archaeological — a man listening to the dead earth.
- **Avoid:** brute or muscular. His power is summoned, not swung.
