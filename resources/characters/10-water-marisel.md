# Marisel — "The Deep Remembering" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Water — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Water read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Water *(primary)* · Dark *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Water · Dark  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Fire  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Light  
> *Attunement: the lightless deep where drowned memory sinks.*

## Concept
A sorrowful tide-witch who drowns her foes in their own past; melancholy and depthless.

## Build & silhouette
Tall and willowy, drifting as though underwater; graceful, with a faintly mournful posture.

## Face & demeanor
A serene sadness, luminous pale-blue eyes, skin with a wet sheen; hair that floats as if submerged.

## Wardrobe & materials
Flowing deep-azure robes that ripple like they're underwater, trailing veils, pearl and abalone accents; barefoot.

## Weapon & props
Ribbons of conjured water and orbs of remembering-tide — no hard weapon; she simply commands the deep.

## Element expression
Water floats around her in slow orbs and coils, faces and reflections glimmering inside it; everything drifts as if submerged.

## Palette
Deep azure `#2A7FB8` with `#4FC3D6` accents; teal-greens and pearl. Wet sheen, translucency, slow drift.

## Key-art pose & mood
Suspended, arms drifting upward, a coil of water rising with pale reflections caught inside it.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** keep it dreamlike, submerged, and mournful, with slow floating motion.
- **Avoid:** splashy or aggressive. She is depth, not waves.
