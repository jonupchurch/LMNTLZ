# Hettamar Ironfall · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Crush — Martial (melee)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Crush read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Crush *(primary)* · Dark *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Crush · Dark  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Pierce  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Light  
> *Attunement: the grim iron of an executioner.*

## Concept
A walking end-of-argument — a grim armored enforcer who settles things with one falling blow.

## Build & silhouette
Massive, dense, and vertical — an iron column; planted and imposing.

## Face & demeanor
Stoic and iron-jawed, shadowed under a heavy helm; unimpressed, immovable eyes.

## Wardrobe & materials
Full heavy bronze-iron plate, riveted and industrial, with a blunt executioner's aesthetic; anvil and rivet motifs.

## Weapon & props
A colossal war-maul or anvil-hammer raised to fall straight down — the final verdict.

## Element expression
A vertical bronze impact-shock and crater bloom on the downward blow, a shock-ring and debris-plume, ground buckling.

## Palette
Forged bronze `#B5732E` with `#E0A15A` accents; iron-grey and rust. Heavy matte metal and dust.

## Key-art pose & mood
Maul raised high in both hands at the apex, about to bring it straight down, a crater already implied beneath.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** emphasize verticality, weight, and finality — the downward slam.
- **Avoid:** flourishing or fast. He is one heavy, certain drop.
