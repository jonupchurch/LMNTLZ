# Grieve — "The Wide Reaper" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Slash — Martial (melee)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Slash read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Slash *(primary)* · Dark *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Slash · Dark  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Crush  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Light  
> *Attunement: the reaper walks with death at his shoulder.*

## Concept
A grim reaper-swordsman who clears a room the way a scythe clears a field; sweeping and inevitable.

## Build & silhouette
Tall and long-limbed, gaunt-powerful, with reach emphasized; looming.

## Face & demeanor
Grim and hollow, shadowed beneath a hood; the tired eyes of a killer; scarred.

## Wardrobe & materials
A tattered crimson-black reaper's coat, long and heavy, with harvest and bone-stitch motifs; worn field-gear.

## Weapon & props
An oversized single-edged blade or war-scythe built for wide horizontal sweeps.

## Element expression
A single vast horizontal crimson sweep-arc cuts the full width of the frame — a mown swath left behind.

## Palette
Crimson steel `#C0313A` with `#F06A72` accents; polished steel and blood-red. Sharp, clean edges.

## Key-art pose & mood
Completing a huge horizontal sweep, a wide crimson arc trailing across several foes at once.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** emphasize reach, sweep, and grim inevitability — go horizontal.
- **Avoid:** cute or nimble. He's a slow harvest of a man.
