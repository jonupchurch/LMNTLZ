# Reyna Two-Rivers · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Slash — Martial (melee)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Slash read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Slash *(primary)* · Water *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Slash · Water  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Crush  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Fire  
> *Attunement: the twin rivers she is named for — a current made of blades.*

## Concept
A dual-blade whirlwind who fights like a current — wide, fast, and everywhere at once.

## Build & silhouette
Athletic and fluid, always drawn mid-motion, with a dynamic twin-weapon spread.

## Face & demeanor
Fierce joy, wind-whipped hair, bright eyes, a warrior's grin; braids strung with river-beads.

## Wardrobe & materials
Layered crimson wraps and light leathers built for spinning motion, twin sheaths crossed at the back; sashes that stream like water.

## Weapon & props
Twin curved blades — river-knives or scimitars — swept in sweeping dual arcs.

## Element expression
Two crossing crimson arcs like converging rivers, a spiraling flurry of cut-lines, motion trails everywhere.

## Palette
Crimson steel `#C0313A` with `#F06A72` accents; polished steel and blood-red. Sharp, clean edges.

## Key-art pose & mood
Mid-spin, both blades sweeping into a crossing double-arc, sashes trailing.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** lean on motion and flow — a current made of blades.
- **Avoid:** static or single-focus. She is constant movement.
