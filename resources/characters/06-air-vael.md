# Vael — "The Falling Sky" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Air — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Air read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Air *(primary)* · Dark *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Air · Dark  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Earth  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Light  
> *Attunement: the storm-black sky she dives from.*

## Concept
A reckless storm-diver who leaps first and lets gravity do the paperwork; an aerial shock-attacker.

## Build & silhouette
Athletic and coiled, drawn mid-plummet; a daredevil's dynamic frame.

## Face & demeanor
A wild grin or gritted focus, storm-grey eyes, hair blown straight back by the descent; a scar or two.

## Wardrobe & materials
A wingsuit-like glider-cloak in dark cyan and silver, a buckled harness, aerodynamic wraps; goggles pushed up on the brow.

## Weapon & props
Uses momentum itself — diving strikes with a heavy dive-blade or gauntlets that hit like a thunderclap on landing.

## Element expression
A cone of rushing wind and static wraps them; lightning threads the trailing air; a sonic-boom ring blooms on impact.

## Palette
Pale cyan `#8FCFE0` with silver-white `#DCE6EA`; high-value, low-saturation, misty. Keep it airy and weightless.

## Key-art pose & mood
Dropping headfirst from above, arms swept back, wind-cone and crackle trailing, the target far below.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** make it kinetic and vertical — a real sense of speed and altitude.
- **Avoid:** static standing poses. Vael is always falling.
