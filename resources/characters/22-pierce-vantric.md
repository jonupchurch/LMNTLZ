# Vantric — "The Threading Spear" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Pierce — Martial (melee)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Pierce read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Pierce *(primary)* · Air *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Pierce · Air  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Slash  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Earth  
> *Attunement: the thin, aerodynamic line — a needle threaded through the gap.*

## Concept
A patient lancer who finds the one gap in a wall of shields; a needle threaded through armor.

## Build & silhouette
Tall, straight, and disciplined — a spear-line of a man, fully controlled.

## Face & demeanor
Focused and narrow-eyed, with an aimer's stillness; clean-cut, a single fixed stare.

## Wardrobe & materials
Trim cobalt-and-steel lancer's armor, streamlined, with a single long pennant; minimal and aerodynamic.

## Weapon & props
A long, fine spear built for one perfect thrust through a seam.

## Element expression
A single straight cobalt thrust-line drives forward to a bright pinpoint, all motion converging on one spot.

## Palette
Cobalt `#3B5BD1` with `#7B93F0` accents; steel-blue. Convergent lines and a single bright point.

## Key-art pose & mood
Fully extended in a lunge, spear driven forward along a straight cobalt line toward one gap in a shield-wall.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** lean on line, point, and convergence — disciplined precision.
- **Avoid:** wide or wild. Everything points forward to a single spot.
