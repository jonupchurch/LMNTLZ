# Ember Saelith — "The First Spark" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Fire — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Fire read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Fire *(primary)* · Air *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Fire · Air  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Water  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Earth  
> *Attunement: air feeds the flame — her fire breathes.*

## Concept
A warm, laughing pyromancer who lights up a room — until the room stops being safe.

## Build & silhouette
Youthful and vivacious, medium build, with an open and expressive posture.

## Face & demeanor
A bright, dimpled smile, warm amber eyes, freckles like ember-sparks; hair like banked coals catching the light.

## Wardrobe & materials
Bright orange-and-gold layered garb with sashes and ribbons that flicker like flame-tongues; light and mobile, edges lightly singed.

## Weapon & props
Bare-handed flame — she cups fire in her palms and throws blooming bursts; perhaps a small lantern-censer.

## Element expression
Living flame dances at her fingertips, sparks trail her laughter, and her hair-tips glow.

## Palette
Molten orange-red `#E8552B` with `#FFB347` highlights; charcoal and ash for contrast. Light should come from within.

## Key-art pose & mood
Tossing a bloom of fire upward with a grin, warm light on her face — the first spark before an inferno.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** lead with warmth and charm, with the danger sitting just underneath.
- **Avoid:** sinister or grim. Her threat is that she genuinely means well.
