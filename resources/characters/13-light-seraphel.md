# Seraphel — "The Verdict" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Light — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Light read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Light *(primary)* · Fire *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Light · Fire  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Dark  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Water  
> *Attunement: her judgment burns even as it reveals.*

## Concept
A judgmental paladin whose very gaze is an accusation; radiant law made flesh.

## Build & silhouette
Tall and upright, armored and regal, utterly unwavering.

## Face & demeanor
Stern beauty, luminous gold eyes, a cold righteous calm; hair like spun light; a faint halo.

## Wardrobe & materials
Radiant ivory-and-gold plate with judicial motifs — scales, verses, wing-etched pauldrons; a tabard cut like a court robe.

## Weapon & props
A blazing greatsword balanced like a set of scales; pillars of light descend as judgment.

## Element expression
A halo and descending light-beams; verdict-glyphs ignite around her; foes cast long, harsh shadows.

## Palette
Radiant gold `#F2C744` with ivory `#FFF6D6`; warm whites. Strong bloom and halo, hard cast shadows.

## Key-art pose & mood
Sword raised as a column of judgment-light falls, gaze fixed forward, shadows fleeing.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** make the light feel like scrutiny — austere, radiant authority.
- **Avoid:** warm or comforting. This is judgment, not sunshine.
