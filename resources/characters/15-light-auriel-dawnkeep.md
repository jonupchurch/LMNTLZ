# Auriel Dawnkeep · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Light — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Light read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Light *(primary)* · Water *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Light · Water  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Dark  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Fire  
> *Attunement: a calm dawn reflected on still water.*

## Concept
The last lantern on the wall — a steadfast guardian of light who simply will not go out.

## Build & silhouette
Sturdy and warm, a sentinel's grounded stance with the shield forward.

## Face & demeanor
Kind but resolute, with tired-around-the-eyes warmth and a gold-flecked gaze; weathered and hopeful.

## Wardrobe & materials
Warm ivory-gold watchman's armor, a lantern-motif tabard, a heavy cloak; everything well-worn and dependable.

## Weapon & props
A radiant lantern-shield and a beacon-mace; he casts protective domes of dawn-light.

## Element expression
A steady warm glow-dome shelters allies, a beacon-flare burns on the shield — the light of a held sunrise.

## Palette
Radiant gold `#F2C744` with ivory `#FFF6D6`; warm whites. Strong bloom and halo, hard cast shadows.

## Key-art pose & mood
Shield raised against a dark tide, a dome of warm light holding, unbroken and steady.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** make his light shelter rather than judge — warm, protective, enduring dawn.
- **Avoid:** harsh or cold. He is the guardian who stays.
