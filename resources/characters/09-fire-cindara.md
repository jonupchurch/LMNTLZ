# Cindara — "Daughter of the Long Burn" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Fire — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Fire read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Fire *(primary)* · Earth *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Fire · Earth  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Water  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Air  
> *Attunement: the coal in the ground; her heat lives in the earth.*

## Concept
The coal, not the flame — a quiet, patient heat-witch whose fire is slow, deep, and inexorable.

## Build & silhouette
Composed and upright, medium build; still and contained, radiating heat rather than throwing it.

## Face & demeanor
A calm, smoldering gaze; dusky skin with a faint inner glow at the veins; dark hair with ember-red underlights.

## Wardrobe & materials
Deep smoke-red robes with banked-orange lining and little ornament; heat-shimmer at the hems; a single slow-glowing pendant.

## Weapon & props
A censer or brazier-staff of slow coals; she conjures creeping ground-heat and delayed detonations.

## Element expression
Heat-haze warps the air around her, embers pulse beneath her skin, and the ground glows faintly where she has stood.

## Palette
Molten orange-red `#E8552B` with `#FFB347` highlights; charcoal and ash for contrast. Light should come from within.

## Key-art pose & mood
Standing serene as a slow ring of ground-fire ignites around her, heat-shimmer rising.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** lean on restraint and banked heat — the patience of a furnace.
- **Avoid:** flashy flame-bursts. She is smolder, not spectacle.
