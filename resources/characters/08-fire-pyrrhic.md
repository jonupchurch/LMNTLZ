# Pyrrhic — "The Glad Ruin" · LMNTLZ Hero Brief

**Type:** Fire — Arcane (magic)
*Art brief for character design. Type matchups (strengths & weaknesses) are in the panel just below; broader systemic lore lives in `../LORE-and-flavor.md`. Keep the Fire read unmistakable — the palette below drives frame, glow, and effects.*

> **Type matchups**  
> **Identity:** Fire *(primary)* · Light *(attuned — lesser)*  
> **Strong to / resists:** Fire · Light  
> **Very weak to — Bane (super-effective vs. this hero):** Water  
> **Moderately weak to — Fault:** Dark  
> *Attunement: his self-immolation blazes near-blinding.*

## Concept
A scorched berserker-mage who wins by having less left to lose than you; he burns bright and self-consuming.

## Build & silhouette
Lean, scarred, and sinewy — a burnt-down candle of a man, all energy and no mass.

## Face & demeanor
A manic serenity: burn-scarred skin, eyes like coals, a too-wide calm smile; ash-white, singed hair.

## Wardrobe & materials
Charred, half-ruined robes and blackened plate flaking to cinder; embers glow through the cracks in his very skin.

## Weapon & props
Self-immolating flame — he wreathes his arms in fire and spends his own vitality for bigger blasts; a cracked ember-brand.

## Element expression
His body smolders from within along fissures of molten light — the more damaged he is, the brighter he burns; ash flakes off him.

## Palette
Molten orange-red `#E8552B` with `#FFB347` highlights; charcoal and ash for contrast. Light should come from within.

## Key-art pose & mood
Arms spread and wreathed in his own fire, grinning amid the blaze he has become — victorious and ruined at once.

## Do / Avoid
- **Do:** make his self-destruction beautiful — molten cracks and an unsettling 'glad' calm.
- **Avoid:** fresh, clean, or heroic-shiny. He's already half-ash.
