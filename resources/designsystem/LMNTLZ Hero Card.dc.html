Why tall in battle
Twelve cards share the board, six per side in the 2/3/1 formation. A tall card matches the standing figure, keeps the face above the fold at every row depth, and leaves the darkened lower third for HP and the five power slots — the only things a player reads mid-turn.
Effectiveness flyout · hover a badge
LIGHT — MAJOR WEAKNESS
Takes +50% damage from Light. Derived from her primary Force, Dark — shared by every champion of the House.
Portrait crop rules
One source image per hero, three crops. Wide: cover at 52% / 12% — full height, figure biased left. Tall: cover at 50% / 8%. Tile: tighter, face in the upper third. UI never overlaps the face — only the gradient does.
