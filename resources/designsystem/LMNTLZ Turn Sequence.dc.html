THE TURN SEQUENCE
One champion's turn · five phases Server-authoritative
Combat resolution

Every turn runs the same five phases, in order.

Initiative hands the turn to one champion. Upkeep resolves what was already on them, the attack is declared and calculated, the defender answers it, riders land, and then the clock ticks on everything that expires. The Court resolves all five before the client is told anything.

ORDER IS FIXED
No phase is skipped — an empty phase resolves as a no-op, not a jump.
STAGE, THEN APPLY
Attack stages its riders; defense decides whether they stick.
A TURN CAN END EARLY
Stun in upkeep, a KO, or a miss all short-circuit to resolution.
DURATIONS TICK LAST
A 1-turn buff cast this turn survives to be used next turn.
