Brand identity system

The nine forces,
one identity.

LMNTLZ (pronounced elementals) is a competitive fantasy battler. Nine damage types — six arcane, three martial — are not a palette decision, they are the brand. This book sets the wordmark, the Elemental Spectrum, the voice, and the rules that keep all of it one system.

The Elemental Spectrum

06

Voice & messaging

Voice
Confident · mythic · a little wry
Speak like a Warden Court herald who has seen this match before. Short declaratives. Concrete nouns from the world — doors, seams, shards, weight. Never hype-speak, never cute.
Elevator
Nine forces, twenty-seven champions, and one rule: every strength is a door. Send six to strike, leave six to stand, and win by reading the doors instead of stacking your own.
Do
“Three of yours bleed to the same Bane.”
Don't
“Uh oh! Your team is super weak!!”
